About WSTA

THEORGANIZATIONALHOME.

West Sound Technology Association is the 501(c)(3) nonprofit umbrella behind West Sound CoderDojo and other regional technology initiatives.

Fostering technology, education and opportunity in West Puget Sound since 2000.

History

BEFORE THE DOJO.

2000

Founded as West Sound Technology Professionals Association to support a vibrant local technology sector.

2008

Renamed WSTA to reflect a broader community spanning technology, business, education, government and nonprofits.

2015

West Sound CoderDojo launches, with WSTA becoming fiscal and collaborating partner.

2017

WSTA transitions toward a foundation model supporting technology and education initiatives.

Mission

AMPLIFY WHAT BUILDS OPPORTUNITY.

WSTA partners with aligned organizations to strengthen the region's technology economy, STEM education and career opportunity — particularly where access has historically been uneven.
