What the LRG does

EXPERIENCE SHOULD TRAVEL.

01

Come see one.

Attend our dojo first and experience how a local group actually works.

02

Choose your path.

Affiliate with the regional group or form your dojo independently. Both paths are valid.

03

Share what works.

Swap ideas, practical lessons and best practices from years of running sessions.

04

Launch locally.

When capacity allows, we may help with first-event logistics or registration — while your local team owns the ongoing work.
