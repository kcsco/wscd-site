BRING THE SUPPORT YOU NEED.

There is no minimum age on curiosity. Younger participants need the parent, guardian, or other support required to participate safely and happily. Adults are welcome to learn too — not merely supervise.

  • Very young makerCome with hands-on adult support.
  • Independent learnerChoose a direction and ask when stuck.
  • Adult learnerRegister and build alongside everyone else.
  • Parent / grandparentYou can code too.
Before you ask

YES, REALLY.

Do we need any experience?

No. Complete beginner through very advanced are all welcome.

Is it a class?

Not in the traditional sense. Sessions are informal, self-paced and project-based. Mentors float the room rather than lecture from the front.

Can we bring a tablet?

A tablet can work as a starter device, but a laptop becomes much more useful for full development work.

What if we do not know what to work on?

That is normal. We suggest project ideas at different experience levels and can help you find a starting point.

Do we have to stay for the full session?

No. Come for what works for you, though registration is still required.

Is it actually free?

Yes. Always.
