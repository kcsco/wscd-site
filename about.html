About

BUILT HERE.OPEN TO EVERYONE.

West Sound CoderDojo began in Kitsap on May 2, 2015 with a simple belief: people learn best when they are given room to explore, make and help one another.

Volunteer-led. Community-supported. Free from the beginning.

What we are

NOT A CLASSROOM WITH COOLER FURNITURE.

People choose projects, move at their own pace, get stuck, ask for help, teach one another, and try again. Coding is part of the story. So are AI, DIY, physical computing, design, collaboration, leadership and the confidence to make something that did not exist before.

Just curious people of all ages showing up to make things.

What we protect

Our lineage matters

LOCAL DOJO. GLOBAL MOVEMENT.

West Sound CoderDojo is independently run locally under West Sound Technology Association while remaining part of the wider CoderDojo / Code Club movement and the charter commitments that made the model worth joining in the first place.

Where we live

WEST SOUND TECHNOLOGY ASSOCIATION.

West Sound CoderDojo was founded by Doña Keating and has operated under the umbrella of West Sound Technology Association since launch. WSTA provides the organizational home that helps keep the dojo volunteer-led, community-supported and locally rooted.
